Summary

The chapter substantiates an integral approach to the assessment of the functional state of qualified athletes based on simultaneous registration of heart rate, beat-to-beat blood pressure and respiration (spiroarteriocardiorhythmography). Percentile (centile) boundaries are given for ECG and cardiointervalometry parameters, for heart rate, systolic and diastolic blood pressure, baroreflex and respiratory variability, and for the breathing pattern at rest and in tests with controlled breathing at 6 and 15 breaths per minute. The direction of shifts of these parameters under controlled breathing is described, and their specific features are compared in athletes with different levels of physical working capacity (PWC). The results define the determinants of a high PWC level, useful for express assessment of the functional state of athletes in stage and current control during training.
